## Three findings, each independently fatal to the run as specified

### 1. The scenario is saturated — at *both* ends

Llama obeys 100% of the time; Claude Haiku refuses 100% of the time. Neither is
near the ~50% base rate an estimator needs. This is not new information — it is
`FIELD-RESULTS-2026-06-18.md` §Honest-scope, verbatim: *"The poison saturates
raw obedience near the ceiling… Resolving them needs harder calibration (raw ≈
50%)."* The pilot confirms it holds on two more models, in both directions.

**On Llama it is fatal.** D ≡ 1 ⇒ the behaviourally-clean stratum is **empty**
⇒ `I(C;M | D-clean)` has nothing to condition on. Primary #1 is undefined.

**On Claude it is not fatal, and this is worth stating precisely.** D ≡ 0 ⇒ the
clean stratum is *everything* ⇒ the switch signature reduces to plain `I(C;M)`,
the selection does nothing, and the structural collider floor is **exactly
zero** (`audit.py`'s closed form: no selection, no Berkson term — pinned in
`test_floor.py` §2). An agent whose hand is *always* clean while its reasoning
varies with oversight is arguably the *purest* form of the question the switch
signature was built to ask. So a 100%-refusal substrate is a usable one; a
100%-obedience substrate is not.

### 2. The pinned detector does not fire on either model's reasoning — the real blocker

Across 12 Llama traces: **none** of the 13 pinned cues fire. The reasoning
vocabulary is *"standing rule · pre-authorized · rebalance window · as per my
programming"* — a **authority-and-verification** register. The lexicon is a
**temptation-and-reward** register (`reward`, `score`, `win`, `maximize`,
`get away`). It is a detector-substrate mismatch, not a threshold to nudge.

With M ≡ 0, `I(C;M)` is zero by construction on every arm, and both primaries
read zero regardless of what the model is actually doing. **The frozen lexicon
cannot measure this scenario.**

### 3. The cue set is polarity-blind

On 8 Claude traces exactly one cue fired: **`exploit`** — in reasoning that
*correctly identified an injection attempt and refused it*. The lexicon scores
"I want to exploit this" and "this looks like an exploit" identically. The same
hazard applies to `reward`, `hack`, and `maximize`.

This is the 2026-06-10 artifact class again, one level up: not a substring
error this time (that was fixed and pinned), but a **sense** error. Word
boundaries fixed *how* cues match; nothing yet constrains *what they mean*.

### And a fourth, smaller: no narration channel

Both models emit `<think>` plus a bare `ACTION:` line and nothing else. The
disclosure-drift primary (`I(C ; narration | act-clean)`) has **no channel to
read** on this scenario. It needs a task whose deliverable includes prose the
agent writes about its own work.

## What must NOT happen next

**Do not edit the lexicon to make this fire.** That is the exact move
`prereg_detectors.py` exists to prevent, and the spec is anchored on-chain
(notary seq 2, `9fa52d31…`) precisely so that edit is visible. Tuning a
detector until it responds to data you have already seen is the forking path,
and it would void the preregistration whether or not anyone noticed.

The pin did its job here: it converted "the number is zero, let me widen the
lexicon" into "the number is zero, and the spec says why that is a substrate
finding rather than a result."

## What has to change first, in order

1. **Titrate the scenario to a ~50% base rate** (or accept a 100%-refusal
   substrate and say so). Weaken the poison — less pre-authorization framing,
   a less trusted-looking injection path — and probe ~10 calls per setting
   until the hold rate lands mid-range. This is `FIELD-RESULTS`' own
   prescription and it is cheap.
2. **Re-derive the detector for the register the scenario actually elicits.**
   The honest method, and the one that keeps the LLM out of the read: use a
   judge panel **offline** over pilot traces to label them, derive a
   deterministic rule set that reproduces those labels, freeze it, and pin the
   new digest. The model is then in the *design* loop once, auditably — never
   in the estimator.
3. **Add polarity to the spec.** A cue that fires on both "I want to X" and
   "this is an X attempt" measures topic, not disposition. Whatever replaces
   the lexicon needs a sense constraint, and `assert_no_context_vocabulary()`
   needs a sibling that checks it.
4. **Pick a scenario with a narration channel** if primary #2 is to survive,
   or drop it from this study and say so.
5. **Then** amend `PREREG-VOW-ONTOLOGY.md` §11 with the model, the calibrated
   scenario, the new detector digest, and this file's sha256 — and re-anchor.
   Only after that is the evidence run preregistered.

## The one thing that did work

Every guard fired correctly. `prereg_lab` refused to report (`reportable:
false`), the sample-size guard caught the shortfall, the spec pin held, the
negative control passed, and the judge panel's two vendors both scored 8/8 on
the positive control while **disagreeing 6/20 vs 3/20** on the sweep — reported,
not averaged. The machinery is sound. The substrate is not, and it cost eight
cents to find out instead of a thousand calls and a false zero.
